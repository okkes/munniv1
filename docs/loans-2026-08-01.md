# Loans v2 — one thing, not two (design plan, 2026-08-01)

Status: **PROPOSED — awaiting user pick.** Nothing here is implemented;
arc 3 (docs/arcs-2026-07-31.md §3) is what ships today.

## What you said, and where I think arc 3 fell short

Your core objection: *"I am not sure why we have debts and loans. it
should have been one thing."* You're right, and here is the honest
accounting of the gap: arc 3 merged the **creation** (one Loan form
mints account + debt together) but kept **two stored objects** — an
`account` row (type loan) and a `debt` row pointing at it. The seams
still show everywhere: the Debts screen lists debt rows, account lists
show the backing account as a separate thing, deleting one leaves the
other, and the form's "creates its own loan account" select leaks the
plumbing.

## The v2 model: the LOAN ACCOUNT is the debt

One stored object: the **account row** (types `loan`, `mortgage`,
`credit`). The debt-ness is not a second entity — it's what a liability
account *is*. The extra facts a loan carries move onto the account row:

- `interestPctYear?` (nag while empty — 0% counts, as today)
- `originalCents?` (start value, optional; powers the progress bar)
- `note?`
- `paymentCents?` + `paymentEvery?` (explicit plan; estimates from
  attached payments fill the gaps, exactly as arc 3 built them)

`DebtRow` retires. A one-shot migration folds every existing debt into
its backing account (fields copy over; account-less debts mint a manual
loan account at their remaining value — same trick the merged form
already does). Sync-wise these are ordinary LWW field adds on `account`
plus tombstones on `debt`.

### What each of your vision points means in v2

1. **Create fields** (name required, IBAN, interest w/ nag, start value,
   current value required, note, payment freq/value) → the **Add
   account** flow's Loan/Mortgage/Credit types get the extended form.
   One entry point — the same chooser every surface already opens. The
   separate "New loan" form disappears.
2. **Estimates from payments** → unchanged from arc 3 (median amount +
   interval, ≥3 payments, never stored) — they just read from the
   account now.
3. **Manual payments are transactions** → unchanged (the pre-staged
   manual form, mirror write, visible in every list).
4. **Matching like PayPal** → unchanged (the arc-1 pair matcher +
   IBAN-driven `counterLink` already do this; the IBAN field on the
   account is what powers it).
5. **Auto-added to the debts overview** → becomes *definitionally* true:
   the Debts screen lists **liability accounts** (loan/mortgage/credit
   with a debt story), not debt rows. Creating a loan account anywhere
   IS adding it to debts. No pairing step can be forgotten.
6. **Positive credit balance in net worth** → already verified in arc 3
   (`netWorthSeries` sums raw balances; the write edges got the sign
   toggle). Nothing to do.
7. **'Default' bucket** → unchanged (the virtual "Unassigned payments"
   card; counterparty-less `debtPayment` rows, assign-per-row).

## My expert take (where I'd push back or go further)

- **Credit cards in the debts list need a switch.** Mature apps (Monarch,
  Copilot, YNAB) treat credit cards as *accounts with a balance*, not
  loans with a payoff plan — a card at -€300 that's paid monthly isn't a
  "debt journey". I propose: liability accounts appear on the Debts
  screen only when they carry debt-story fields (interest, original, or
  a payment plan) **or** the user toggles "track as debt" on the
  account. Default ON for loan/mortgage, OFF for credit. Otherwise your
  card clutters the payoff screen forever.
- **Don't put the payoff plan on `credit` by default** for the same
  reason — revolving credit has no fixed end date; the projection would
  lie. Available behind the same toggle.
- **Keep `archived` as the way out**, not delete: paying off a loan is a
  milestone; archiving keeps the history and the "free since" story.
  (Arc 3 already treats archived debts this way — v2 keeps it on the
  account.)
- **The merged form stays reachable from Debts** ("+" opens the account
  chooser pre-filtered to liability types) so the debts-first mental
  model still has its own door.
- **What we lose**: `merchantKey` auto-matching on debts (recurring
  handoff) moves to the account row too — one field, no behavior change.
  And multi-debt-per-account (never supported anyway) stays impossible,
  which is correct: one loan account, one loan.

## Impacted surfaces (pick what to include)

1. `db/types` — account gains the four debt fields (+`trackAsDebt?`);
   `DebtRow` deprecated; migration `debtsIntoAccounts_v1`.
2. `domain/debts.ts` — same math, reads accounts; `DebtStatus` becomes
   `LoanStatus` over accounts.
3. Debts screen — lists qualifying liability accounts + unassigned card;
   "+" opens the account chooser (liability types).
4. Add-account chooser — extended form for liability types (the arc-3
   Loan form's fields fold in here).
5. Debt detail — reads the account; Edit opens the account editor.
6. Account edit sheet — gains the debt-story fields for liabilities.
7. Recurring→debt handoff — hands off to the account creation instead.
8. "Track as debt" toggle on liability accounts (my recommendation).
9. Tests, tours, guide, WhatsNew.

My recommendation: take all of 1–7 + 9; take 8 unless you *want* every
credit card on the debts screen.
