Design answers + implementation plans for the large requests from the 2026-07-31

individual arcs can be approved/trimmed before implementation.

## 1. Transfers: the duplicate pair, counter-creation, and the placeholder question

**How mature apps handle it** (YNAB, Monarch, Copilot, Lunch Money — they all
converge): a transfer is ONE logical event with TWO legs. They auto-match
opposite legs between owned accounts (same |amount|, dates within ±1–3 days),
LINK them, exclude both from spending, and in the all-accounts feed collapse the
pair into a single "A → B" row. Per-account views keep the individual leg —
that account's running balance needs it.

**Design for munni:**

1. **`transferPeerId`** on TransactionRow + TxMetaRow: the mirror transaction's
   id, written on BOTH legs (cheap lookups, LWW-safe via writeTxTransform).
2. **Matcher** (`application/transferMatch.ts`), same skeleton as
   `matchPaypalPairs`: candidates are rows linked to an own account
   (`linkedAccountId`) or IBAN-matched between own accounts; pair rule =
   opposite signs, same |amount|, ≤2 days apart, different accounts; ambiguity
   skips (paypal precedent — never guess). Runs beside `linkAllCounterparties`
   after import/sync/review-confirm.
3. **Display**: transactions list renders a paired transfer as ONE merged row
   ("A → B", swap icon, single amount) when both legs are visible in the
   current space; filtering by account shows the individual leg. Overview and
   budgets need no change (transfer-family types are already excluded).
4. **Manual counterparty** (user request): labeling a tx as transfer to a
   MANUAL account offers two doors — **"Create the matching transaction"**
   (default: writes the mirror row on the counter account, links both legs) or
   **"Find the matching transaction"** → a link screen reusing the
   reimbursement pattern (suggestion segment scored by exact amount ±0, ±2
   days, right direction; then the full searchable list).
5. **Linked/imported counterparty — the placeholder question.** My
   recommendation: **do not build placeholder transactions.** A fabricated row
   must be deduped against the real import later (fragile matching), corrupts
   the counter account's balance timeline when the bank books a different
   date/amount, and no mainstream app ships it — the industry answer is the
   **one-sided transfer**: the leg you have is already correctly typed and
   excluded from spending; when the other side syncs, the matcher links it and
   the pair collapses. What the placeholder was really for — "see that the
   other side is coming" — becomes a subtle **"awaiting counterpart" pill** on
   the one-sided leg (derived state: transfer-typed + counter account is
   feed-backed + no `transferPeerId`), plus a review-time suggestion when a
   candidate lands. Self-heals, fabricates nothing.
6. **Verdict on the current concept**: the counterparty-derives-type model is
   sound and matches the market. The real gaps were (a) pair linking/merging
   (this arc) and (b) the forced counterparty (categories arc below).

### 1b. Cross-space transfers — the "family contribution" (user question 2026-08-01)

The case: a monthly payment from a private-space account to a joint bank
account that is attached ONLY to the family space — and should stay there.

**State today**: nothing called "family transfer" exists. Worse, the private
space's counterparty picker only offers accounts that space sees, so the
joint account is unreachable and the row is stuck as a plain expense —
inflating private spending every month.

**What others do**: YNAB (the closest model to spaces — separate budgets)
declares cross-budget money an EXPENSE in the sender and INCOME in the
receiver, because budgets cannot see each other. Single-pool households
(Monarch, Copilot) call it a plain transfer because everything is one
universe. munni sits between — spaces partition the BOOKS, but the account
pool underneath is global — so it can do better than both.

**Design v2 (user revision 2026-08-01) — the FUNDING type, no cross-space
references at all:**

- Spaces NEVER reference each other's transactions or accounts (user
  rule: the cross-space picker/matching complexity is not worth it). The
  arc-1 pair matcher is scoped to WITHIN one space's books.
- New stored type **`funding`** with its own kind (no counterparty by
  design — the other side keeps its own books). The private space labels
  the €500 as Funding: it leaves the space's balances (net worth drops
  honestly) without polluting expense categories — "high chance the
  majority of the monthly cost would be just this".
- The family space types the joint account's incoming €500 as Funding
  too — the pot was funded, that is not income.
- **Overview card gains two tiles** (SHIPPED 2026-08-01): **Funded** —
  net money put into other spaces' pots this period, green when
  contributing, red when drawing back out — and **Repaid** — net debt
  paid off this period, red when a period borrowed more than it repaid.
  Both signed and color-coded; both excluded from budgets/spending as
  transfer-family always was.
- Locked category subs land with arc 2's door wave, under the locked
  main **"Funding"**: **"To another space"** (debit) / **"From another
  space"** (credit) — direction-true in every space's own books.
- Why not the YNAB way (expense-out/income-in): with both spaces on one
  device the user would double-count — the contribution as private
  spending AND the joint account's purchases as family spending. Funding
  records consumption once (where it happens) while still SHOWING the
  monthly flow on its own tile; anyone who wants a contribution to read
  as personal spending can still flatten it (arc 2's optional
  counterparty: a standard expense with a "Household" category).

**Impacted surfaces**: db/types (+field, invariants), application/transferMatch
(new), counterLink, TransactionsScreen (merged row), TxRow, TxDetail (peer
line + unlink), TxKindSheet (create/find doors), new TransferLinkScreen,
review confirm hook, tests, tours (transactions), guide.

## 2. Categories: locked transfer-family doors + optional counterparty

**Locked categories** (mirror of the Reimbursement door): four system mains,
no user subs, no edits, hidden from budget/trends pickers, the SUB picked
automatically by the row's sign — like `REIMBURSED_ID` today.

| Type | Debit sub (money out) | Credit sub (money in) |
|---|---|---|
| transfer | Transfer out | Transfer in |
| saving | Set aside | Taken out |
| investment | Invested | Sold off |
| debtPayment | Repaid | Borrowed |
| funding | To another space | From another space |

Existing builtin transfer subs (`transferOut/In/cashWithdraw/cashDeposit`)
fold into this; retired ids tombstone via the catalog machinery.

**Optional counterparty** — endorsed. Mechanic: the kind sheet's transfer
family no longer FORCES the counterparty picker. Three exits:
- pick a counter account (today's path — type derives from the account);
- **"No counter account"** → the user picks the specific type
  (saving/investment/debt payment/transfer) directly; the row gets the type +
  the locked sub by sign, no `linkedAccountId`;
- or stay standard: the credit-card example ("label the €100 top-up as
  Electronics") is a STANDARD expense with a real category — already legal;
  the flatten option means we never push those users into account-modeling.

Counterparty-less `debtPayment` rows feed the loan arc's "unassigned payments"
bucket. Honest limitation to accept: without a counter account those rows
can't move any balance — they are reporting labels, which is exactly what the
flattening user wants.

**Impacted surfaces**: domain/categories (locked ids), catalog bake +
tombstones, CategoryPicker gating, ManageCategories, TxKindSheet (no-counter
exit), review roll-back rule (drop), TxFormSheet, budgets/trends exclusions,
writeConfirmation auto-sub, migration for existing rows, tests, tours, guide.

## 3. Loan redesign (plan + my position per point)

The core move: **merge "debt" and "loan account" into one creation flow** — a
single "Loan" form that mints the backing account and the debt together
(today's two-object dance was the friction).

Per your list — agree, with notes:
- display name ✓ · bank account number → `account.iban` (optional — and it
  powers auto-matching, so worth asking for) · interest rate optional with the
  weekly nag (now correctly throttled — a `.value` bug made it fire every
  open; fixed today) · start value → `originalCents` (optional) · **current
  value required** (the truth anchor; stored negative internally) · note →
  new `debt.note` · payment frequency + value optional.
- **estimateFrequency/estimateValue**: derived, never stored — median interval
  and median amount of attached payments, labeled "estimated from payments",
  used wherever the explicit fields are empty. Standard practice.
- **Manual loan payments ARE transactions**: a pre-filled manual tx (funding
  account picked, counterparty = the loan account, type auto `debtPayment`,
  category auto = locked "Repaid"); appears in every transactions list ✓.
- **Matching**: the same transfer matcher (arc 1) links funding-account debits
  to loan-account credits; the IBAN field extends it to bank-fed loans.
- **Loan account ⇒ debts overview** automatic (1:1 already; the merged form
  removes the manual pairing).
- **Positive credit-card balance in net worth**: verified — `netWorthSeries`
  sums raw balances, so +€1000 on a card already adds correctly; only the
  WRITE edges forced liabilities negative, and that got the −/+ toggle today.
  Nothing else to change. The debt view clamps an overpaid loan to
  "remaining 0", which is right.
- **Default debt bucket**: a VIRTUAL "Unassigned payments" card on the debts
  screen (sum of counterparty-less `debtPayment` rows) with per-row "assign to
  a loan" — computed, not a stored debt, so no sync/migration baggage.
- Critical notes: the payoff projection must follow the chosen payment
  frequency (today it assumes monthly); the interest nag stays weekly and
  never blocks; frequency/value estimates need ≥3 payments before they claim
  anything.

**Impacted surfaces**: db/types (debt.note, paymentEvery; account.iban on
manual loans), DebtFormSheet → merged LoanForm, DebtsScreen (+unassigned
card), DebtDetail (estimates line), domain/debts (projection per frequency,
estimates), TxFormSheet prefill door ("add payment" from debt detail),
transfer matcher hookup, migration (existing debts keep working), tests,
tours, guide, WhatsNew.

## 4. Space creation form + private lock

Create sheet grows to: name · icon + color (reuse the settings avatar
editor) · budget period · ledger currency · history start — the last three
REUSING the exact sheets Settings already opens, prefilled with defaults
(period month/1st, EUR, start = −2 months per arc 5). Everything except name
has a default, so "type a name, press create" still works — which also keeps
Mina's create-space act step valid (her suggestion prefills the name; tour
copy gets a light touch-up mentioning the extra fields exist).

**Private lock**: new `SpaceRow.inviteLock` (default ON for new spaces).
While locked, the invite/share surfaces render disabled with an explainer;
unlocking is an explicit toggle in space settings (owner only). Synced field,
LWW like the rest.

**Impacted surfaces**: SpacesScreen create sheet, spaceDefaults, Mina steps
copy (+ suggestions), SettingsScreen (lock toggle), invite/share flow
(SpaceSettingsScreen members block), db/types, tests, tours (spaces), guide.

## 5. Start-date mechanics

Already true today: bank/imported rows are stored in FULL and only filtered at
display time per attachment (`accountLink.historyFrom`); nothing is lost. The
arc closes the gaps:

1. **Space-own rows join the filter**: `visibleTransactions` gains the same
   date gate for the space's own (manual/legacy) rows against
   `space.historyStartDate`.
2. **Create default**: history start defaults to **today − 2 months**; the
   create sheet hints "start small — 3 months at most — so review stays
   manageable".
3. **Import preview + result** say it out loud: "N transactions are older than
   this space's start date — they stay stored but hidden."
4. **Manual transactions BEFORE the start date are refused** with an inline
   error and a one-tap "move the space start to this date" action.
5. **Changing the date** (Settings): moving it **newer** shows a warning with
   counts — "X hidden (bank/imported — recoverable), Y manual transactions
   DELETED"; manual rows tombstone, feed rows just hide. Moving it **older**
   warns "N transactions will surface and may ask for review" and suggests
   stepping ≤1 month at a time.

**Impacted surfaces**: db/joined (own-row gate), SpacesScreen create,
spaceDefaults (2 months), SettingsScreen history sheet (counts + warnings),
import preview/result copy, TxFormSheet validation + quick action, invariants
(manual date ≥ start), tests, guide, Mina (none — her demo data is recent).

## 6. Notifications center + bell badge

- **Inbox**: device-local, per-identity meta list (the `txSeen` pattern —
  never synced): `{id, kind, ts, payload}` appended by: What's-new (on version
  change), recurring due reminders, the debt rate nudge, and budget-over
  events. The OS push/SW notifications keep firing as today; the inbox is the
  in-app record. Works identically offline and online (meta is per identity).
- **Bell**: the sheet gains two tabs — **Notifications** | **Activity** —
  activity stays exactly what it is (audit trail, explicitly NOT notifications
  per your rule). What's-new moves INTO the notifications list (row opens the
  existing What's-new sheet); the Home banner retires.
- **Badge**: a count pill on the bell (unread = entries newer than
  `notifSeenAt`, capped at 9+). Opening the Notifications tab stamps
  `notifSeenAt` → badge clears. No per-row read state in v1 — tab-open marks
  all seen (simplest honest model; per-row read adds chrome without value at
  this volume).

**Impacted surfaces**: NotificationsBell (tabs + badge), application/
notifications (new meta store), HomeScreen (banner removal), recurring.ts +
sw.ts appenders, WhatsNew wiring, tests, tours (home), guide.

## 7. Per-space attention indicators

On the switcher rows AND the manage-spaces rows: a compact accent pill
"N to review" (per-space `needsReview` count via the join rules — computed
when the sheet opens, per space) and a red dot when any budget is over its
limit (per-space `budgetStatus`, catalog via the `counterLink` recipe). The
Home avatar (switcher trigger) gets a dot when any OTHER space needs
attention. Counts compute lazily at open — no background cost.

**Impacted surfaces**: SpaceSwitcher, SpacesScreen, a new
`application/spaceAttention.ts` (count helpers), HomeScreen avatar dot,
tests, tours (spaces), guide.

## 8. Multiple offline profiles

Lift the one-per-device rule: `addOfflineProfile` mints always (today it
silently returns profile #1 — a landmine once multiples exist);
`adoptOfflineProfile`/Go-offline drop the refusal; the chooser always offers
"Add another profile". Creating the **2nd+** profile first shows a fullscreen
Mina (thinking pose, DebtHandoffInterstitial layout): profiles are fully
separate worlds — SPACES inside one profile are how you split family/business/
private — with **Continue** / **Go back**. Per-profile isolation (db name,
lock key) already scales by construction; the copy that asserts one-per-device
(`offline.oneProfileHint`, `goOffline.blocked`) gets rewritten.

**Impacted surfaces**: offlineProfiles.ts, LoginScreen, GoOfflineScreen +
goOffline.ts, new interstitial, i18n, tests, guide.

## 9. Naming: the two account screens

Proposal — name them by SCOPE, not source:

- Global screen: **"All accounts"** (`acct.financialAccounts`) — icon
  `bank-outline`. NL "Alle rekeningen" · TR "Tüm hesaplar".
- Space screen: **"This space's accounts"** with the space name as subtitle
  (`space.financialAccounts`) — icon `wallet-outline`. NL "Rekeningen van
  deze ruimte" · TR "Bu alanın hesapları".

Rationale: "Financial Accounts" twice was the confusion; "All …" vs "This
space's …" answers where-am-I in the title itself, survives translation, and
the two icons (bank vs wallet) carry the distinction into settings rows,
tours and the guide. Rollout: retitle both screens + their settings rows,
swap icons, update tours/guide/tests in one sweep.
